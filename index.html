Read this first. Providing liquidity exposes you to impermanent loss: if the KNTQ price moves, you end up with more of the token that fell and less of the one that rose. The KNTQ/USDC pool is very thin (a few hundred dollars), so prices can jump on small trades and your share of the pool can be large. In the KNTQ/HYPE pool both tokens move against the dollar. Narrow ranges earn more while in range but stop earning (and sit 100% in one token) when the price leaves them. This page is a tool, not financial advice. Start small.
